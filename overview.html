PROJECT OVERVIEW

Subliminal is a collaborated work by the enthusiastic Minecraft modpack multiplayer players that also have a taste for role-playing, and directed by unsupportedfiletype, a.k.a. File.

The project, in its early stage, used to be an What If. What if the prophecy didn't happen. What if the Fun Gang were never chosen to be the hero. What if Alvin suddenly had the idea of making a new Hometown in a distant land. However, due to File not being very liquid on his finance, the Minecraft server which it took place on was shut down.

During the wait period, Subliminal had made a revamp on its lore, and next will be its modpack, and eventually, the experience it will bring us.

Now, although Subliminal is still standing incomplete, its modpack is in rapid development. Please stay tuned - for that we will soon be writing our own stories, with the one white pen that rewrites fate.
