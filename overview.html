PROJECT OVERVIEW

“An interesting, yet seemingly improbable phenomenon has occurred.

The original prophecy spoke of three heroes destined to save the world: The Human, The Monster, and The Prince from the Dark.

But what happens in a world in which the promised heroes never existed?

Subliminal takes place in an alternate universe of DELTARUNE, where Kris, Susie, Ralsei, and the familiar inhabitants of Hometown never existed to begin with.

Without them, the events that were supposed to unfold could never take place. Expectations of events to play out in accordance to the prophecy had been shattered.

The balance between Light and Dark remains as was in DELTARUNE and strange events will still remain to surface.

The spot of the missing heroes shall be taken upon by none other then the ordinary (aka YOU).”

- unsupportedfiletype


Subliminal is a collaborated work by the enthusiastic Minecraft modpack multiplayer players that also have a taste for role-playing, and directed by unsupportedfiletype, a.k.a. File.

The project, in its early stage, used to be an What If. What if the prophecy didn't happen. What if the Fun Gang were never chosen to be the heroes. What if Alvin suddenly had the idea of making a new Hometown in a distant land. However, due to File not being very liquid on his finance, the Minecraft server which it took place on was shut down.

During the wait period, Subliminal had made a revamp on its lore, its modpack, and even where we start with. No more "just a simple church there". No more "Yes but this shit is filled with irrelevant things". We will start anew, and with the things we get we'll progress again.

It's still a What If, but what we'll have will be far grander.

Although Subliminal is still standing incomplete for now, its exclusive mod is in rapid development. Please stay tuned - for that we will soon be writing our own stories, with the one white pen that rewrites fate.
